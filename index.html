KARLITOSDENTI

Alternative Electronic · Experimental · Indie · Left-Field Pop

KarlitosDenti (Carlo Denti) is a 16-year-old Italian artist, songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist from Sicily. His music moves between alternative electronic, experimental pop, indie, rock, jazz and hip-hop, blending contrasting influences into a sound with a distinct identity.

The Project & Biography

The Artist

KarlitosDenti began studying music and playing piano at the age of three. He has developed a strong musical ear, including absolute pitch, and is currently studying at a music-focused high school in Sicily.

He writes and produces his own material and collaborates with emerging artists from the USA, Canada, Mexico, England and South Africa.

The Project

ACT I, ACT II and ACT III are three albums forming a single narrative cycle — a conceptual trilogy combining experimental music with an evolving fictional story.

The story follows Dave, Brian and Cadett, three agents working for a government intelligence agency running into a psychological aftermath through separation and reunion.

Recommended Listening Order

ACT III → ACT I → ACT II

The three albums are designed to be listened to in this order, gradually revealing how the story connects.
